M0 is a preparation release. The v0.2.2 research checkpoint extends it: M1 is resolved with a formal blocker, M2 and M3 are completed with verified negative results, Hypothesis H4 (graph separators) and the Calibrated Scaling Benchmark (n=12..20) are fully executed and documented, Milestone M5 (Useful Transfer) is completed with an exact Weighted Model Counting engine for network reliability and risk analysis, and high-core parallel Cube-and-Conquer lookahead was evaluated across 302 runs on a 64-core AMD EPYC server (evaluating 194,165,819 search nodes and mapping complete topological hardness gradients across grid and barbell topologies). Neither a performance gain on random CNF nor the upstream complexity theorem is established. Preserved evidence grounds all findings.
